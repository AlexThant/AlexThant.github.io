Projects

D3Q27 · CUDA · AMR

Cumulant LBM ROV Solver

GPU-accelerated cumulant lattice Boltzmann solver with adaptive mesh refinement and interpolated bounce-back boundaries, built for maneuvering simulations of underwater vehicles in a translating-and-rotating body-fixed reference frame.

ANCF · Contact Mechanics

ANCF Cable–Vehicle Coupling

Coupled tether–vehicle solver using the absolute nodal coordinate formulation, with ellipsoid closest-point contact projection and a consistent tangent for hydrodynamic drag.
