Bio

I’m an Assistant Professor at The University of Osaka, working at the intersection of multibody dynamics, computational fluid dynamics, and high-performance numerical methods. My research focuses on the dynamics of complex marine systems—from ROVs and mooring systems to offshore renewables—with an emphasis on efficient simulation of coupled rigid–flexible bodies and fluid–structure interactions. I develop computational methods and algorithms that make large-scale, high-fidelity simulation practical. More recently, I’m extending these ideas beyond the ocean to bio-inspired morphing wings, tethered airborne wind energy systems, and electric solar sails (E-sails).

Currently based at Marine Hydro-Science and Engineering Lab.
